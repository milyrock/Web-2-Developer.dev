About Me

I’m Alex, a full stack developer and SEO specialist with 6 years of field experience helping clients to solve problems by code and optimizing websites to rank higher in search engines.

Over the past 6 years

I've worked remotely for agencies, consulted for startups, and collaborated with talented people to build products for companies and businesses around the globe ranging from marketing websites to complex solutions and enterprise apps with a focus on fast, elegant, and accessible user experiences. I'm calmly confident, inherently curious, and eternally working on improving my chops one design problem at a time.

Design

I'm probably not the "graphic designer" positioned behind a design tool ex. Illustrator shaping pixels, but I design. You will find me absorbed in stylesheets tweaking font sizes and contemplating layouts, and designing logic and algorithms on the back-end that most people don't see, but it creates an accessible user experience while staying fashionable.

Engineering

As a full-stack developer, I'm on both sides, front-end and back-end. On the front-end, you will find me architecting CSS in and out of JavaScript and building slick animations with GreenSock (GSAP). On the back-end, you will find me building an architecture of an application, maintaining the server-side of web applications, QA, and supporting the team in any way I can.
