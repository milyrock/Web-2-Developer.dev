A Little About Me

Hi! I’m Alex and I solve problems for awesome people!

My journey on the web began as a freelance SEO specialist (Google Certified).

Providing SEO service ever since, until one of my friends introduced me to Python and algorithms. I immediately got hooked, watched some youtube videos, took some courses online, and started providing services as a python developer within two months!

Solving complex problems with code requires a lot of things, so I had to learn and apply it to my work constantly. Before I knew it, I was providing all kinds of developing services I became a full-stack developer.

As a full stack developer, I’m comfortable with variety of frameworks. Typically I will be digging in with django, architecting CSS in and out of JavaScript and building slick animations with GreenSock (GSAP).

Whatever the tools used, I flourish in bringing ideas to life. I take pride in crafting innovative solutions with polished user experiences. I'm obsessed with a keen eye for design, accessibility, performance, and well-structured code.

As a self-taught developer and SEO specialist, I had the opportunity to work with amazing people worldwide with the fantastic on-field experience of 6 years.

I’m a part-time Educator. I’ve mentored 30 students to help them become skilled developers; most of them are working in the field worldwide.

I’ve recently started studying/reserching on cyber security and I’m fascinated by it. I provide bug Hunting and fixing
issues, malware,virus & backdoor, firewall making and DOS/DDOS attack protecting.

Away from the keyboard in my "spare" time, I like to travel a lot. I live in a town with a big lake, so I swim whenever I find the time, it helps to relax the mind and calm the nerves. I also love to fish, if you don’t see me at home or online, I’m probably fishing!
